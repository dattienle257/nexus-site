Getting started

  • Today — your day: the schedule of time blocks, the focus timer, your priorities, habits and training.
  • Calendar — plan the day in time blocks, by day, week, month or year. Touch and hold empty space, then drag, to block time (click and drag on the Mac).
  • Personal — habits, gym, pelvic floor training, meditation, to-dos, the journal and ideas.
  • Work — tasks (priority matrix and board), projects on a Gantt chart, your team, and the work record with the focus timer and its dashboard.
  • Settings — calendar and alerts, focus and blocking, iCloud sync, backup and Recently deleted, notifications, training, sample data and appearance.

Want to see it with data first? Settings → Sample data → Load sample data adds example time blocks, tasks, projects and habits. Delete them any time from the same place.

Focus stretches and blocking

Start a stretch from Today, from a time block on the calendar, or from Work → Record. While it runs, Safari can block the sites you list (Settings → Focus & blocking → Blocked sites), and on the Mac the apps you choose can be hidden. The Safari extensions must be switched on once in Safari's settings; the app shows the exact path. When the stretch ends, an entry for your work record opens with its start and end filled in.

iCloud sync

Settings → iCloud sync → Sync with iCloud is on by default on every device; turn it off there if you want a device to keep its data to itself. Sync needs the device signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive enabled. Your data lives on your devices and in your own private iCloud, never on our servers; if you turn sync off, the data on the device stays.

Backup and restore

Settings → Data → Export backup saves a .json file with everything. Import backup restores it: merge adds the file's items and updates the ones you already have to the file's version (everything else stays); replace wipes and reloads (a safety snapshot is taken first). Backups exported before the app was renamed import the same way.

Deleted something by mistake?

Settings → Data → Recently deleted. Habits, tasks, projects, routines and sessions wait there for 30 days.

Apple Health and heart-rate sensors

Both are optional and only used during gym workouts. Grant or revoke Health access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Hubday, and pair a Bluetooth chest strap or a watch's heart-rate broadcast in Settings → Training → Gym → Heart rate sensor. What Hubday reads from Health and from the sensor stays on your device.
