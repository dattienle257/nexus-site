Contact: dat@tonkinboutiquehotel.com. Include your device and OS version; a screenshot helps.

Getting started

  • today — your dashboard: focus tasks, today's training, habit checklist, momentum.
  • personal — habits, plus the gym and Kegel trainers under # training.
  • work — tasks (matrix · board · calendar) and projects (Gantt planner).
  • config — theme, font, iCloud sync, backup and the recycle bin.

Want to see it with data first? config → data → load sample data adds example habits, tasks and a project. Delete them any time.

iCloud sync

Turn on sync.icloud in config on each device. Sync needs the device signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive enabled. Data lives only in your private iCloud; if you turn sync off, the data on the device stays.

Backup and restore

config → data → export backup saves a .json file with everything. import backup restores it: merge keeps existing items and adds the file's, replace wipes and reloads (a safety snapshot is taken first).

Deleted something by mistake?

config → recently deleted. Habits, tasks, projects, routines and sessions wait there for 30 days.

Apple Health and heart-rate sensors

Both are optional and only used during gym workouts. Grant or revoke access in Settings → Health, and pair a Bluetooth chest strap or watch broadcast from the workout screen.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. Nexus has no accounts, analytics or servers.
